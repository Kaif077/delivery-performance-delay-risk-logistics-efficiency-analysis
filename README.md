# Delivery Performance, Delay Risk, and Logistics Efficiency Analysis in Global Supply Chain Operations

## Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing delivery performance, shipment delays, logistics efficiency, and delay risk in global supply chain operations. The analysis aims to identify operational bottlenecks, delayed delivery patterns, high-risk regions, and factors affecting shipment performance.

## Problem Statement
Despite having detailed order and shipping data, organizations often lack:

- Clear measurement of on-time vs delayed deliveries
- Understanding of why shipments are delayed
- Visibility into high-risk regions, markets, and shipping modes
- Diagnostics to explain delivery delay risk

As a result, logistics teams operate reactively instead of preventively.

## Objectives
- Analyze delivery performance and shipping delays
- Identify causes of shipment inefficiencies
- Detect high-risk regions and markets
- Evaluate logistics efficiency using data analysis
- Predict late delivery risk using machine learning models
